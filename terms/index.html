Kiebitz is a local-first chess companion for desktop and Android. Analysis, training and data storage run on your devices; the provider operates no server that receives your games.

You can only enter into a Kiebitz Plus contract if you have full legal capacity. The account is personal; passing it to third parties is not permitted. You can delete your account at any time on the account page or in the app; a running subscription must be cancelled first.

07Term and cancellation

The subscription runs for one month and renews automatically by one further month unless you cancel. You can cancel at any time with effect from the end of the current billing period — no notice period, no reason required.

Subscriptions bought through Stripe can be cancelled on the account page under “Manage subscription” or by an unambiguous statement to support@kiebitz.dev; subscriptions bought through Google Play are managed in the Play Store. After cancellation Kiebitz Plus stays active until the end of the already paid period and then falls back to Kiebitz Free. Your local games, analyses and training data remain unchanged on your devices. Either party’s right to terminate for good cause remains unaffected.

The cancellation function “Cancel contracts here” is also available: reachable without signing in, it shows the request number and the time of receipt on the page and sends a confirmation of receipt by email.

§ Withdrawal

Withdraw from a contract

Declare the withdrawal from your Kiebitz Plus contract here — without signing in and without an account. The period and the consequences are set out in section 8 of the terms.

  1. Withdrawal button: the link “Withdraw from a contract” sits on the Kiebitz Plus page, which the footer of every page links to. You have already followed it.
  2. Confirmation page: fill in the details and submit with “confirm withdrawal”. You then see the request number and the time of receipt.
